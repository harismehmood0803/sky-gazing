Lunar Surface

🌑 Outer Layer of the Moon

The lunar surface is the outer part of the Moon that we can see from Earth.

🪨 Covered with Regolith

It is blanketed by a layer of dust and crushed rock called regolith.

🌋 Craters Everywhere

The surface has many craters formed by meteor and asteroid impacts.

🌄 Maria and Highlands

It has dark flat plains called maria and bright rough areas called highlands.

☀️ No Atmosphere

The Moon has no air, wind, or weather on its surface.

❄️ Extreme Temperatures

Daytime temperatures can reach 127°C, while nighttime can drop to -173°C.

🚶‍♂️ Low Gravity

Gravity on the lunar surface is one-sixth that of Earth.

🧑‍🚀 Human Exploration

Astronauts first walked on the lunar surface during Apollo 11 in 1969.

🌌 Reflects Sunlight

The Moon shines because its surface reflects light from the Sun.
