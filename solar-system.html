Solar System

🌍 Definition

The Solar System is a group of the Sun and all the objects that revolve around it, including planets, moons, and asteroids.

☀️ The Sun

The Sun is a huge ball of hot gases and the center of the Solar System — it provides heat and light to all planets.

🪐 Planets

There are eight planets in the Solar System: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

🌎 Earth

Earth is the only known planet that supports life, with water, air, and a suitable atmosphere.

🌑 Moons

Many planets have moons that orbit around them — for example, Earth has one moon, and Jupiter has over 90.

☄️ Other Objects

The Solar System also includes asteroids, comets, meteoroids, and dwarf planets like Pluto.

🔭 Formation

It was formed about 4.6 billion years ago from a giant cloud of gas and dust.

🛰️ Orbits

All planets move around the Sun in fixed paths called orbits due to the Sun’s gravity.

🌌 Exploration

Scientists study the Solar System using telescopes, satellites, and space missions to learn more about space.
