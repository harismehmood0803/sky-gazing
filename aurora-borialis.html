Aurora Borealis

🌈 Definition

Aurora Borealis is a natural light display seen in the night sky, mostly near the North Pole.

☀️ Cause

It happens when charged particles from the Sun collide with gases in Earth’s atmosphere.

🎨 Colors

The lights appear in beautiful colors like green, pink, red, blue, yellow, and violet.

🗺️ Location

Aurora Borealis is mostly visible in northern countries such as Norway, Canada, Finland, and Iceland.

🧠 Name Origin

The name “Aurora Borealis” was given by Galileo Galilei and means “northern dawn.”

🧭 Scientific Reason

The Earth’s magnetic field guides solar particles toward the poles, creating glowing lights.

🌙 Best Time to See

The best time to see auroras is during dark, clear winter nights.

🌏 Southern Counterpart

In the Southern Hemisphere, the same phenomenon is called Aurora Australis or Southern Lights.

💫 Cultural Beliefs

Many ancient cultures believed auroras were messages or signs from gods and spirits.
