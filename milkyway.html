Milky Way Galaxy

🌌 Our Home Galaxy

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System, including Earth.

⭐ Hundreds of Billions of Stars

It’s estimated to have 100–400 billion stars and possibly just as many planets.

💫 Spiral Shape

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy, with a central bar-shaped structure and several spiral arms.

🌍 Location of the Solar System

Our Solar System lies in the Orion Arm, about 27,000 light-years from the galactic center.

🌀 Size

The galaxy spans about 100,000 light-years in diameter.

⚫ Supermassive Black Hole

At its center is a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*.

🌠 Rotational Motion

The Milky Way rotates; it takes the Solar System roughly 225–250 million years to make one orbit around the galactic center.

🌃 Part of the Local Group

It belongs to a small cluster of galaxies called the Local Group, which includes Andromeda and the Triangulum Galaxy.

🔭 Visible Band of Light

When viewed from Earth, the Milky Way appears as a milky band of light across the night sky, made up of billions of distant stars.
